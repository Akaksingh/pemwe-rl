## 1. Positioning

Working title: *Simulation Throughput and Learner Scaling for Reinforcement-Learning Control of a
PEM Electrolyzer under Variable Renewable Input: A Computational Performance Study*
(manuscript draft: `paper/main_track2.tex`).

Thesis the evidence can carry: stack degradation takes years to observe, so control strategies must
be screened in simulation; this paper characterises **how fast and how cheaply** that screening can
be done, where the bottlenecks are, and how not to measure them. The controller comparison is the
*workload*, not the headline. Scope statement (agreed in chat): no physical stack was operated; the
simulator is a pre-screening tool; conclusions concern relative controller behaviour and
computational performance, not absolute stack lifetime.

## 4. Scenario generator (built and run)

`scripts/make_scenarios.py` regenerates the 1-minute series from the shipped hourly means with the
noise amplitude varied (hourly means preserved to ~1e-10 W; `original` reproduces the shipped file
to 9e-10 W). Realisation 0 of each scenario:

| scenario | ramp-1min p99 (% of 1 MW) | major events/day | low-power intervals/day | acf(lag 5 min) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| shipped / original | 11.9 | 17.5 | 2.7 | 0.40 |
| low variability | 6.2 | 1.3 | 1.4 | 0.52 |
| high solar | 14.8 | 29.3 | 3.5 | 0.41 |
| high wind | 20.6 | 51.2 | 5.5 | 0.25 |
| combined high | 22.0 | 61.4 | 6.4 | 0.30 |

Independent noise realisations of one scenario are nearly identical in these aggregate metrics, so
the single shipped realisation is not what drives them. No measured 1-minute data exist here, so the
**original scenario itself is unvalidated**; capacity factor is unchanged across scenarios
(0.440–0.443), so differences are due to variability alone.

## 7. Reviewer objections to prepare for

| Objection | Prepared answer |
|---|---|
| "One workstation is not HPC." | Agreed; framed as a scaling and bottleneck analysis on workstation-class hardware with honest limits; we explain, not just report, the efficiency drop (clock, not code). |
| "No hardware validation." | Stated up front; simulator is for pre-screening; claims are relative. |
| "Why does the learned controller not beat the simple rule?" | Reported as measured, with the update-ratio and discount-factor findings and the pilots. |
| "Degradation model is not validated; calibration anchor is one paper." | Limitation; the sensitivity study shows coefficient uncertainty changes life by ≈16×, which is the point. |
| "Synthetic sub-hourly variability." | Parameterised and swept; independent realisations nearly identical; real data named as future work. |
| "Speedups hide different work." | This is our own finding (C1): we report update ratio and time-to-quality. |
| "Statistics." | Days, seeds and repeats named as units; bootstrap intervals from raw CSV; no significance claims. |
